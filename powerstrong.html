LESS FRICTION BETWEEN THE STEPS

Every handoff.
One shared context.

A lead asks a question. A coach follows up. A purchase becomes a training plan. The work should stay connected.

PowerStrongAI keeps the contact at the center, carrying shared context through the pipeline, inbox, payments, and client delivery. I built the interfaces, API, data model, and integrations that connect those handoffs.

Discover & prioritize→Understand & respond→Collect & onboard→Deliver & adapt
