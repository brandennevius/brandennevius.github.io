Product tour

Decide what needs attention

Start on the dashboard to see hot leads, clients at risk, and upcoming meetings. Open the Intelligence queue to see why a lead was flagged and what to do next.

Track leads and clients through the pipeline

The sales pipeline shows who is at each stage. Alex Morgan is in Active coaching. Open his contact record to see the current stage, upcoming meeting, and training coverage.

From Instagram inquiry to reply draft

Jules asks about coaching in an Instagram DM. The inbox shows the original message and an editable consultation reply. The coach can also review the same thread from Jules’s contact record.

From a saved offer to checkout

Coaches save a reusable offer in Billing and generate a checkout link. The Strength Reset example shows the saved package and the matching $199 monthly subscription in Stripe test checkout.

Turn a training template into a client schedule

Build a reusable program with workouts laid out by week. Assign it to a client with a start date. Alex’s record shows Foundations of Strength and the workouts ahead.

Turn an intake into an assigned follow-up

Choose the form that starts the workflow, then set the actions it should run. For Northstar, the intake tags the contact, assigns Casey a task, and moves the lead to Consultation.

Publish a page that leads to intake

Build a landing page with the site editor, then publish it with a button that opens the coaching intake. Northstar uses its landing page to explain the service and give prospects a clear next step.

Collect the details a coach needs

Manage intake forms, questionnaires, and agreements from one place. The Northstar intake asks for contact details and training goals before a consultation.

Copilot actions

See how the assistant responds to a client update and prepares tasks, offers, and pages for coach review.

A client message turns into a reply and a training recommendation.

01Review the reply→02Review the program change
  1. 01

    Draft a reply from the inbound message

    Lena’s hotel gym has no cable machine. Reply Assistant reads her message and drafts a response that addresses the equipment change.

    Read the message and draft

    Lena’s message

    I’m traveling for work next week and the hotel gym has dumbbells but no cable machine. Can we swap the seated cable row for a dumbbell row while I’m away? I can still train on my usual days.

    Reply Assistant draft

    Absolutely, we can swap the seated cable row for a dumbbell row while you're traveling. Just aim for three steady sessions as planned, and let me know how the dumbbell row feels. Safe travels!

    Programming review uses the same message and Lena’s active plan

    ↓
  2. 02

    Review the suggested exercise swap

    Programming review suggests a dumbbell row in place of the seated cable row. The proposed operation covers all upcoming workouts that match, so the coach can narrow the change to Lena’s travel week before editing the plan.

From a coaching idea to a working checkout.

One connected path through Copilot, coach approval, Billing, and Stripe test checkout.

01Describe the offer→02The offer is saved in Billing→03The checkout is ready
  1. 01

    Describe the offer

    Copilot turns the request into a reviewable package proposal.

    Read the exact prompt and reply

    Your prompt

    Create a monthly coaching package called "Strength Reset" for $199 per month. Description: Three strength sessions per week with a weekly coach check-in. Prepare it for coach review.

    Copilot’s reply

    Prepared offer draft. Strength Reset — $199/month. Review and approve the package draft to create it.

    Coach reviews and approves

    ↓
  2. 02

    The offer is saved in Billing

    ↓
  3. 03

    The checkout is ready

A coaching offer becomes an editable landing page.

01Review the proposal→02Open the draft
  1. 01

    Give Copilot the page brief

    Tell Copilot who the page is for, what the offer includes, and what visitors should do next. Copilot prepares a proposal for the coach to review.

    Read the prompt and reply

    Coach’s prompt

    Create a landing page draft for Strength Reset. Audience: adults getting back into strength training. Offer: three strength sessions per week with a weekly coach check-in. CTA: Fill out the coaching intake. Style: simple and practical.

    Copilot’s reply

    Prepared site draft. Landing. Review and approve the draft to create it in Sites.

    The coach approves creating the draft

    ↓
  2. 02

    Edit the page in Sites

    Approval creates a draft in Sites. The coach can revise the generated blocks and preview the page before publishing.
