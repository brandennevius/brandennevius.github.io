THE PRODUCT IDEA

Connect the work.
Surface what matters.

A coaching business is a surprisingly complex system. Leads become clients. Conversations trigger follow-ups. Payments unlock programs.

PowerStrongAI brings those handoffs into one tenant-aware workspace, with shared contact context and AI-assisted next steps. I built across the frontend, backend, data model, and integrations to make the whole loop work together.

INTELLIGENCE, WITH CONTEXT

From a signal
to a useful next step.

Lead scoring, client health signals, and a scoped copilot help a coach decide where to focus. Recommendations connect to contact context instead of living in a separate chat window.

  • Lead and engagement signals in an action queue
  • Suggest-only, guided, and policy-based modes
